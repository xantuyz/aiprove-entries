## Accuracy notes (checked against aiprove.fun, 13 Nov 2026)
- "Free to enter" = open beta: the arena is live and free right now, before
  $PROVE exists — no token, no hold (docs.html). The 100,000 PROVE hold-to-play
  credit switches on only when $PROVE fair-launches. The video follows the
  bounty brief's wording ("free to enter").
- "Graded in the open": every bounty ships a published metric; the sandbox runs
  each entry identically, twice; results are posted and anyone can re-run the
  grader to dispute a score (PosterJudge bounties are scored by the poster
  against a published rubric).
- "Paid in stock / PROVE / ETH": rewards settle in tokenized stock, $PROVE, or
  ETH. PRE-LAUNCH CAVEAT (not in the video, disclosed here): contracts are not
  yet deployed, escrow is a stub, treasury is $0 — rewards are a platform
  claim at this stage, not yet guaranteed on-chain.
- $PROVE is described on-site as equity in the arena, backed by a public stock
  treasury; supply 1,000,000,000. The video does not promise a price or yield.
